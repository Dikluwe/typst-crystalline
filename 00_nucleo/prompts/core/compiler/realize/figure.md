## Pure preview and final preparation

inspection; omitted kind through effective `figure.kind`, then auto inspection.

for explicit Name/Element and an absent supplement callback. Validation is pure,
respects the semantic visitor's opaque boundaries and never prepares a child

Preview synthesizes kind only. It performs no callback, child realization, source
evaluation, registry mutation or allocation of Location/Context/occurrence
identity. Pure RAM cloning/reconstruction is permitted by ADR-0029/0030;
`01_core/src/compiler/realize/figure.rs:86–107` reconstructs a preview Figure in
an Arc. Preview kind is the sole newly certified synthesized selector field;
supplement-dependent preview selection remains outside this contract.

Final preparation resolves kind under the effective styles after matched
show-set deltas. Explicit constructor kind/supplement override inherited values.
A prepared alias retains its resolved fields. Final getters, fields, queries and
show arguments expose the resolved kind and supplement. Preserve Source and
Located transport and the prepared original's complete entity data.

Auto supplement localizes the exact native Table, Raw and Image identities through
the existing locale table. A custom Name or other nonlocalizable kind with
numbering enabled reports `please specify the figure's supplement`; default
numbering is enabled, while explicit None disables it. A disabled nonlocalizable
Auto supplement is known empty Content. Explicit None means no supplement;
explicit Content retains that Content.

A supplement Func receives the first exact-kind semantic descendant, or the
original body if none matches. Execute it with effective styles, contextual
access and no Location; retain its original argument span and traces. Convert

emitted. Restore context, current Location and the previous `defer_show_rules`
flag on success and failure. A reached own-hidden or captionless Figure still
prepares its outer supplement before replacement; a parent-hidden Figure never
prepares. Do not promise callback-once execution mechanics or reuse callback
results across validation transactions.

Figure set supplements retain their value under `figure.supplement` and the
exact last named value span in companion `figure.supplement-origin`, an existing
Value::Args with empty positional/named arguments. This private carrier contains
span metadata only; execute no arguments/callback and expose no user field.
A Func's first definition origin cannot replace the last value occurrence span.
