## Measured causes and correction

1. Adjacent rows emitted both identical bottom and top border runs. A bottom run
   is now suppressed only with an identical full declarative Stroke/endpoints
   and proof that the next row remains on the same page. The later top is kept;
   page edges, gaps, partial overlaps and differing strokes retain their policy.
   The existing page-break predicate is shared privately, without predicting
   cell-body execution or changing pagination.
2. Absolute PDF stroke coordinates produced different device-space edge rounding.
   Shape lines and non-Tiling straight two-point paths now establish a local
   origin with five-decimal endpoints/deltas inside their existing graphics scope.
   Negative directions retain the physical start and affine composition.
3. Generic local paths negated Y a second time after their Group established
   Y-down. The clipped-line control disappeared completely in PDF. Its local
   endpoints/control points now use the established frame; clipping and sibling
   state remain intact. Other top-level curved paths and Tiling transport keep
   their existing path representation.

The [factorial PDF experiment](../../lab/results/construction/diagnostics/painting-r5-2026-10-24/pdf-experiments/receipt.json) isolates duplicate
paint and coordinate frame: neither repair alone closes both resolutions; both do.
The [residual experiment](../../lab/results/construction/diagnostics/painting-r5-2026-10-24/residual-experiments/receipt.json) independently
shows the same coordinate cause for straight Path, closing its remaining three
high-resolution pixels. Moving fills before borders did **not** close the small

These experiments are diagnostic copies; production performs owner-local layout
and export corrections, not output-file postprocessing.
