## A / D1 — trailing weak pagebreak creates an extra blank page

**Measured:** the direct fixture below produces two pages in Tekt debug and
release, one in pinned vanilla. Removing the final newline does not change that
result. A single-iteration loop also reproduces it.

```typst
#set page(width: 300pt, height: 400pt, margin: 20pt)
#set text(font: "DejaVu Sans", size: 11pt)
One #pagebreak(weak: true)
```

The multipage fixture produces 21 versus 20 pages; the Context fixture produces
11 versus 10. Both retain the complete visible words; Tekt's final page is empty.
A 20-iteration control with no final break produces 20 pages in all three binaries.
This rules out a release-only or newline-only explanation.

Evidence: [follow-up receipt](../../lab/results/tekt-vs-vanilla-2026-10-23/render-followups/receipt.json),
[minimal fixture](../../lab/results/tekt-vs-vanilla-2026-10-23/render-followups/weak-direct.typ),

[Context fixture](../../lab/results/tekt-vs-vanilla-2026-10-23/corpus/context.typ).

**Source evidence, not a complete causal diagnosis:**
`01_core/src/compiler/layout/pagebreak.rs` requests a transition without inspecting
`weak`. Its [current L0](../prompts/core/compiler/layout/pagebreak.md) explicitly
records that limitation. Update that owner before changing behavior; do not claim
the present L0 already requires weak-break collapse. Audit the finalization and
page-run interactions under their own owners (`finish.md`, `page_run.md`,
`page_commit.md`, `mod.md` in the same prompt directory).

**Acceptance:** RED→GREEN on direct/loop/newline variants and both larger fixtures;
preserve strong pagebreaks and deliberate blank pages, parity-directed breaks,
content after a weak break, repeated weak breaks, PageRun boundaries, Context/page

because it looks empty: page materiality and language intent must justify it.

## B / D2 — init success notification can panic

**Measured:** successful local `init` with stdout redirected to `/dev/full`
creates the project, then Tekt exits 101 with a captured panic:
`failed printing to stdout: No space left on device (os error 28)`.
Pinned vanilla exits 0 in this probe because its success messages use stderr.
The channel difference must remain explicit; this is a Tekt robustness defect,
not evidence that vanilla provides checked stdout here.

Evidence: [CLI receipt](../../lab/results/tekt-vs-vanilla-2026-10-23/robustness-cli/receipt.json),
case `kind=product`, `name=init-success`, `failing_channel=stdout`;
measured 13:37:10.609789–13:37:10.612956 UTC.
The receipt includes exact argv, local template, environment, messages and
before/after files. With both channels failed, exit 101 is observed but stderr
cannot provide a captured panic message.

**Cause at publication:** `04_wiring/src/init.rs` uses two `println!` calls for
success. Its [L0](../prompts/wiring/init.md) explicitly leaves those candidates
outside the prior stderr correction. Private `run_init` already returns
`io::Result<ExitCode>`. Inspect checked output patterns and their owners before
implementation. Project validation/download/transactional writes belong in L3.

**Reproduction:** use template `@local/stderr-probe:0.1.0` from the preserved
fixture, a fresh destination, isolated `XDG_DATA_HOME`/`XDG_CACHE_HOME`, and
`NO_COLOR=1`. The recorded destination already exists; rerunning into it would
test a different error. Copy only the local package fixture into a fresh lab
directory; do not require a registry/network download.

**Acceptance:** healthy success bytes/order/channel and exit 0 remain intact;
healthy initialization failure keeps its existing message and exit 1. Actual
notification write/flush failures propagate as ordinary operational errors
under the current CLI policy, without panic. Cover full stdout, broken pipe and
failed stderr. Already-created project files survive notification failure; do
not roll back or retry initialization to compensate. Do not move success messages
to stderr as an incidental workaround: the current L0 preserves stdout.
