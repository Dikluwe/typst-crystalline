## Contract reconciliation

including prepared/isolated Cell and Source origins, SmartQuote target-time
resolution, typed transforms, math.matrix, counter action reshaping, Page/bundle
slices, logical page numbers, compact carriers, Display/FromIterator and affine text size. Original
receipts and manifests remain unchanged. Later implementation alone was never
used as approval.

Active limitations remain explicit: missing or incomplete runtime API/Route
specification, old public proposals whose approval evidence was not located,
partial accessibility and math/selector scopes, isolated versus positioned Cell
projections, and general parity/performance claims unsupported by fresh evidence.
A preserved pending requirement is not newly authorized by translation. Old
oracle measurements remain tied to their original pins; this work does not
recertify them against the current vanilla target.

Nucleus IDs, claim IDs and strengths, schema, dependency edges and owner
cardinality are preserved. Only English title/statement text changes; consumer
pins and forward lineage seals were updated using the pinned linter’s effective
hash framing. The integrity check confirms executable Rust is byte-identical
after removing its forward seal. All 109 nuclei retain an empty dependency list.
