# Evaluation L0 sanitization history

## Scope and provenance

This is an English retrospective diagnostic, not a materializable L0, approval,
nucleus or productive owner. It concerns only
`00_nucleo/prompts/compiler/eval.md` and the prompts under
`00_nucleo/prompts/compiler/eval/`. No global sanitization is asserted.

The documentary review used HEAD
`6ef8b6986e74de7d5cc8f7b7e009868fc0daa5f5` with a dirty working tree. The
eval hub already contained observation/stdlib-registration extraction changes;
the observation prompt family already existed as untracked English documents.
They were used as the baseline, not discarded or replaced from HEAD. The
per-document SHA-256 identifiers below identify those actual input bytes, not
merely the committed revision. Historical measurements made against earlier
states remain historical and are not reproduced by this documentary operation.

Current clauses were checked against the consumers' declared @prompt ownership,
definitions and relevant implementation paths. New source anchors identify live
consumer boundaries; prior upstream file:line readings remain evidence. Old
crystalline offsets, chronology and state records are retained here rather than
being presented as current consumer locations. The reference register preserves
the original recorded coordinates, which may refer to older snapshots.

## Historical decisions and corrections removed from L0 narrative

- The earlier extraction records (P96.1/P109 and P1002/P1009/P1011/P1012/P1013)
  described successive module separation and Engine threading. Their result is
  a set of exclusive consumers with static dispatch/reexports, not a perpetual
  instruction to repeat an extraction. File-size/function-count inventories and
  session-specific execution instructions were removed from normative prompts.
- Observation extraction separated private Same/Different/Unproven comparison
  from EvalContext, contextual request capture and replay. The hub's old math,
  BlockHeight, Boxed and image-alt comparison amendments now belong to the
  observation family. Content stays a closed exhaustive enum; public equality
  and repr are separate observers, not substitutes for this relation.
- Stdlib registration extraction moved global composition to the registry owners.
  Earlier hub narratives about P1292 place.flush, P1293 grid/table short names,
  P1300 color globals, P1307 encoder namespaces, P1402–P1405 outline helpers,
  P1413 raw.line, P1414 par.line, P1471 PDF/HTML callable kinds and P1473 nested
  namespaces explain that composition's history, not a second normative registry
  in eval. The eval hub retains acquisition of inputs, fresh base scope, std/global
  wrapping and pure forwarding facades.
- P1300 recorded extra bare/std hsl/hsv/linear_rgb aliases versus the pinned
  vanilla global constructor set. The old compatibility gate sentence is not a
  new permission to remove bindings. Current registry owners retain that decision.
- P1305 distinguished public Module name from lexical binding name: global is
  stored on the stdlib object, while the binding remains std and a normal std.typ
  import remains std. Earlier repr-only relabeling was refuted by reexports.
- P1215 and P1308 corrected numbered expression Source availability and preserved
  private function origins. A historical build stamp could remain on an older
  HEAD; the overlay, not a version string or guessed offset, makes traces causal.
- P1307-R3/R4/R5 and P1308 replaced lossy Args views with occurrence transport,
  preserved spread/sink/With origins and distinguished With concatenation from
  Args+Args collision removal. Encoder/panic/CSV/abs and later typed wrapper span
  transport accumulated as closed native identities, not name-based heuristics.
  The old proposal and pending-gate labels are not a statement that these
  already-existing consumers are absent now.
- P1307-R5 introduced authoritative realized-content snapshots for field access,
  equality and repr. The old raw Heading fallback was an insufficient control;
  numbering differences and label/location independence explain the separation
  of public field-set equality from strict internal causal observation. CBOR's
  textual fallback changed its string deliberately; it did not become structured
  Content serialization or evidence of general CBOR parity.
- P1285 textual regex changed from one whole-node recipe call to matched-slice
  calls. P1494 later refined the older per-node boundary restriction to realized
  text groups of one lexical snapshot/style chain. Preserving raw ContentBlock
  whitespace required a narrower value-versus-realization distinction.
- P1494's recorded sequential-identical-literal-rule witness yielded
  foo/EARLY/LATE upstream and foo/EARLY/EARLY in the measured crystalline state.
  It was separate precedence debt, not closure evidence for whitespace/group
  matching. The supplementary fixture SHA-256 was
  `161c06c311c616ebe730be2f9ff5332eb50c736737d47cb1f121de678a700575`.
  Local CJK show over an unnormalized fragment likewise remained a combined debt.
- P1286 aligned smartquote quote configuration and alternative selection but did
  not authorize moving markup quote resolution to layout or reconstructing lost
  SmartQuote provenance from curly glyphs.
- P1031 refuted the P1007 premise that contextual state reads in show rules were
  forbidden. The cited documentation establishes implicit style context and
  location context for locatable show rules. A heading witness observed
  got:(1,)|Um and got:(2,)|Dois upstream; the earlier crystalline state rejected
  the implicit read, and an explicit context wrapper silently yielded empty data.
  P1037 located the latter cause in pre-show introspection and subsequent
  substitution, not the pure termination loop. Neither old measurement is proof
  that the present pipeline is fixed. Moving the introspection phase requires
  its own owner and ADR-0127 decision. The termination argument cannot reuse the
  disproven state-read prohibition.
- P1339 native-element selectors, ordered filter groups, filtered counter keys,
  typed function.where and callback observation recorded both execution order
  and language identity. P1341/P1342 strengthened test-only producer/With/body
  attribution without adding normal-build instrumentation or fake body functions.
- P1395 lexical bare-math call precedence refuted hardcoded abs/sqrt priority.
  Its later nonfunction calibration corrected ASCII x to mathematical 𝑥 and
  distinguished repair hints from a separate upstream span annotation that the
  simplified diagnostic carrier cannot express.
- P1290/P1305 calibrated tuple/array shape, width and long-array repr-only elision.
  P1397 and P1399 restored explicit math-style wrappers and opaque context().
  Resource ceilings, run durations, old build states and agent instructions are
  operational history, not morphology contracts. Unmeasured non-ASCII width and
  erased smartquote provenance were not granted general parity.
- P1401–P1415 developed contextual selector/outline/raw.line/par.line contracts.
  Older 'not yet supported' or 'awaits approval' statements coexist in the input
  with implemented consumers and later approvals. They were retained as history,

- P1425–P1451 carried math presence, canonical identity and bare/qualified
  convergence through syntax, constructors, observation and repr. Earlier
  MathDelimited/MathUnderover/glyph approximations and tests freezing them were

- P1462–P1469 added lexical format set rules and their progressively extended PDF

## Ownership reconciliation

The eval hub owns orchestration, request logging/replay and entrypoint facades.
Bindings and operators hubs own only their static delegation/reexport boundary.
Child schemas/algorithms remain in their child prompts. Observation/math owns
math structural comparison; observation/elements owns image/box comparison.
Table owns normalize_table_cell only; table numbering stays in rules and native
table/figure/PDF construction stays outside that cast. font_dict and termination
are called by rules but do not make the eval hub the owner of rules.rs.

The snapshot nucleus remains pinned in call_dispatch, field_access, repr and
equality; selector_matching delegates projection rather than inventing a second
snapshot contract. Existing eval-core, feature-gate, observation-relation and
attachment-slot nuclei and their SHA-256 pins were not changed. Test prompts
name only their exclusive test consumer; their parent-module wiring is not a
second consumer licensed by the test prompt.

## Pending issues and authorization boundary

- A targeted lineage reseal remains necessary after changing L0 bytes. No
  productive Rust/header, test, ADR or nucleus was edited, and neither global nor
  targeted resealing was run. Hash do Código metadata and existing pins remain
  unchanged; V5 drift is expected, not a claim of failed runtime preservation.
- Conflicting historical approval/proposal wording cannot establish a new gate
  decision. Existing routes were documented, not newly authorized. Any request
  to remove compatibility bindings, promote isolated methods to first-class
  values, add a public carrier/signature/default or move eval/introspection/layout
  phases requires the appropriate owner and ADR-0127 direction.
- Combined local CJK show/normalization, sequential identical-rule override,
  generic math spread limitations, full nonfunction span annotations, contextual
  termination justification, general Content/CBOR morphology and non-ASCII width
  parity remain bounded obligations. This sanitation did not repair or retest
  them and did not promote Unknown to success.
- Exact diagnostic literals, including existing Portuguese runtime diagnostics,
  were not translated as user-visible behavior. Normative English prose does not
  authorize changing those messages or their severities/hints/traces/spans.
