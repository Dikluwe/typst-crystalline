# Batch E independent language audit

## Measurement and provenance

The initial evidence-only audit phase made no productive or L0 edits and ran no Cargo command. After that audit, the parent explicitly expanded this agent’s ownership to `stdlib/loading/encoding`: its separate phase-1 receipt records the L0 and tests-only edits; the parent subsequently observed three semantic REDs plus one preservation GREEN in `baseline-run/core-red.log` and authorized the bounded encoder implementation. ## Controls and error preservation

- Raw omitted versus explicit `block:false,lang:none`, Empty/Sequence children (corrected sequence probe), Matrix explicit delim/align/augment/gaps, Cell auto/none, Caption explicit position/separator, marked Heading, and typed transform strict methods are individually recorded. Nested Equation legacy defaults can make a whole-object comparison differ without refuting the outer field presence.
- Direct JSON/YAML and query-of-Metadata carrying Cell/Matrix/Caption/Raw/Empty preserve those values in the bounded cases. These query controls exercise actual source and serialization without pretending that nonlocatable Cell/Matrix are directly queryable.
- Direct `table.cell` and `math.mat` query selectors correctly reject as nonlocatable in both. They are expected negative controls, not successful serializer tests.
- Query missing-field filtering returns an empty array; one-result missing field errors; two/zero result cardinality errors occur before missing-field lookup in both formats. Their raw diagnostics remain in query-receipt. An identity heading show rule retains queried body across the reachable carrier path.
- Initial invalid Sequence concatenation `[a][b]`, named OutlineEntry level, and chained `func()[body]` examples were rejected by both; corrected expression probes are separate. They must not count as positive coverage. Direct unlocated Heading inside rendered OutlineEntry fails the oracle location precondition; the located-heading attempt then fails the additional outline-context precondition. The separate generated-entry probe uses `outline()` to supply that context; all attempts are retained.
- Invalid Cell colspan:auto and stroke:auto remain errors in both; unknown Equation field and unset Box width error bodies match. Product `math.equation[x].at("numbering")` and omitted block retain additional presence/diagnostic limitations. No expected error was weakened to turn a probe green.

## Unknowns and refuters

The source-facing carrier probes do not uniquely identify all four internal carrier variants, especially a synthetic Styled→Realized.visual→Equation versus Located.body path. No black-box equality certifies that internal reachability; independently owned Rust tests are needed for those exact carrier constructions. This audit does not claim a failing source case for each internal carrier. The ordinary Styled and Sequence controls are mandatory negative boundaries.

Equation omitted block is currently always Set in the product but unset when omitted in the oracle. Constructor-style identity and outer Styled opacity need separate evidence; public representation changes require their own ADR-0127 classification. The audit recommends no public API or pipeline-phase change.

The findings would be refuted by a replay on a source-verified candidate where the same probes observe the required language values/errors without breaking the controls. These receipts describe the accepted batch-D binary only; source edits made concurrently by the other agents have not been compiled or measured here. This audit neither accepts candidate code nor closes all seven V16 obligations. Structural compile-negative validation and final L0/build/lineage gates remain the parent's work.
